For international applicants, HPI lists the selection weighting as **51%
bachelor's final grade, 15% GRE Quantitative Reasoning, and 34% special
subject-related achievements**. Meeting the entrance requirements is
separate from being selected.

1.  **Email HPI admissions now**: ask whether B.Tech Civil Engineering
    can qualify as a "related major" given substantial professional
    software-development experience.
2.  Ask whether all required 14/15/15/10 credit points must come from
    the bachelor's transcript, and whether professional training or
    other coursework can count.

    descriptions, credit or lecture-hour information and grading scale.
    Map modules to HPI's four subject areas without guessing
    equivalencies.

    immediately and verify accepted English C1 proof.
5.  Consider the GRE General Test, particularly Quantitative Reasoning,
    if time permits.

**Deadline warning:** Today is 13 November 2026 and the deadline is 1
December 2026. HPI says VPD processing averages 4--6 weeks, so timing is
tight. Contact HPI and uni-assist immediately; do not assume a pending
VPD or APS can be submitted after the deadline.
